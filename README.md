# 🐸 Frog Species Habitat Analysis

This project explores the relationships between frog species, provinces, continents, and the temperatures of their habitats. By analyzing this dataset, we aim to understand how environmental factors influence frog species distribution across different geographical regions.

---

## 📘 Overview

Amphibians are highly sensitive to environmental changes, making them excellent indicators of ecosystem health. This project uses a dataset containing frog species information alongside habitat temperature, province, and continent data to uncover insights such as:

How habitat temperature varies across continents.

The most temperature-tolerant frog species.

Regional diversity of frog species.

Correlations between species distribution and climate conditions.

The analysis is performed using a Jupyter Notebook (FrogSpecies.ipynb), leveraging Python’s data science ecosystem.

---

## 🧠 Objectives

- Understand how temperature influences frog species distribution.

- Identify which provinces and continents host the greatest diversity of species.

- Detect possible patterns or correlations between geographical location and habitat temperature.

- Provide visual insights to highlight environmental relationships.

## 📊 Key Insights (Replace with Real Findings)

- Tropical regions exhibited the highest diversity of frog species due to moderate to high temperatures.

- Some species adapted to cooler climates were concentrated in temperate zones.

- The continent with the widest temperature range also hosted the most species variation.

- Temperature showed a moderate correlation with frog diversity — biodiversity peaked in moderate temperature zones.
